Our Story

Long Before Beyond Had a Name

When my oldest daughter was four, her pre-K teacher encouraged me to seek an autism evaluation. A diagnosis followed. I remember feeling lost and scared, hearing what she might never do when what I needed most was help seeing what was possible.

Long before I knew the language of ABA, I learned as her mother that meaningful learning does not have to look the same for every child. Play, daily routines, movement, conversations, and everyday moments can all become opportunities to learn.

I never believed a child should have to sit at a table simply because that is where learning is expected to happen. What matters is engagement, useful learning, and progress toward meaningful, individualized goals.

How We Support Children & Families

Beyond Adventures & Growth™ is preparing to provide individualized, in-home Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) services focused on meaningful skill acquisition and functional goals that matter in everyday life.

Learning does not have to happen at a table.

ABA can be taught through play, movement, preferred activities, daily routines, and real-life experiences when those settings fit the child and the goal. Structured teaching may be useful for some skills, but it is not the only way to learn. The focus is engagement, useful learning, progress toward individualized goals, and helping skills carry over into everyday life.
