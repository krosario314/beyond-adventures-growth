Family Guidance & New Jersey Resources

Practical caregiver support and a clearer starting point for finding help.

Support through Beyond

Family guidance within an agreed ABA plan may include listening to your priorities, explaining goals in everyday language, demonstrating a planned support, practicing together, and reviewing what feels manageable at home. Caregiver coaching is a partnership—not an expectation that you become your child’s full-time therapist.

An everyday example

If getting ready feels difficult, you can bring that concern to the clinical team. Together, you can discuss the routine, the child’s needs, and possible supports. The clinician can explain and demonstrate the agreed approach, invite questions, and review whether it helps.

New Jersey starting points

These are outside programs and resources, not services automatically provided by Beyond.

  • New Jersey Early Intervention System: For developmental concerns before a child’s third birthday, call 888-653-4463 to request help getting started. Evaluation and assessment guide eligibility and an Individualized Family Service Plan (IFSP). NJEIS information for families.
  • PerformCare / Children’s System of Care: Call 877-652-7624 for information about developmental-disability eligibility and available supports. Eligible families may access respite (temporary caregiver relief), assistive technology, certain home or vehicle modifications, educational advocacy, and limited summer-camp assistance. Family Support Services require a separate application after eligibility; services depend on program criteria, need, funding, and availability. Explore services and application information.
  • Special Child Health Services: County case management units offer resource and referral assistance to families of children and youth with special health care needs, from birth through age 21. Find the state program and county contacts.
  • Special education and parent support: Your school district’s Child Study Team is a starting point for evaluation questions. NJDOE provides the PRISE parental-rights guide and information about SPAN parent training and support. Explore NJ school and parent resources.

How Beyond can help you take the next step

Bring your questions about routines, services, and where to turn. We can discuss what falls within Beyond’s planned support and point you toward relevant public resources. The responsible agency determines eligibility and authorizations. Beyond’s participation in a state program or insurance network must be confirmed separately.
