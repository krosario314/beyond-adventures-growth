# Beyond Adventures & Growth Website Development Notes


## Future Clinical & Facility Roadmap

- Planned future addition: a pediatrician relationship/service pathway intended to help families access appropriate medical evaluation more efficiently when clinically and legally appropriate. Do not advertise this as available until the physician arrangement, scope, scheduling, privacy, billing, licensure, and operational requirements are finalized.
- Planned future addition: a physical Beyond Adventures & Growth clinic. Current public language should continue to describe the initial model as in-home services until a clinic location and launch details are confirmed.
- When these items become ready, update the website, intake pathway, insurance/payment information, privacy practices, and service-area language together so families are not given conflicting information.
