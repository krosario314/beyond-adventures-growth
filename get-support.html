Preparing to welcome families

Beyond is preparing its intake process. Secure online intake and client accounts are not open yet. Service availability, eligibility, and next steps will be confirmed individually when intake opens.

Please do not send diagnoses, insurance cards, IEPs, medical records, or other private documents through general email.

Your experience matters

What has helped your family feel supported? What has been missing? If you could shape the support you receive, what would you want it to look like?

When secure intake opens, we want to make space for that conversation. We will listen to your priorities and explore what Beyond may be able to offer, as well as other resources that could help.

For now, keep these reflections for your own planning. Please do not send private family stories or health information through general email.

What the intake journey will look like

  1. Make a connection

    A brief inquiry will help us understand your service location, how to reach you, and the kind of support you are exploring.

  2. Talk through the fit

    We will explain current availability, the service setting, and whether the next step with Beyond may be appropriate.

  3. Complete secure intake

    When available, a secure system will guide you through consent forms, relevant records, and any insurance information needed.

  4. Understand next steps

    The team will explain any assessment, authorization, scheduling, or other requirements before services can begin.
