Insurance & Payment

Clear information before you begin

Understanding coverage is part of planning for support. We want families to know which questions to ask before making a commitment.

Participating plans have not been confirmed

Beyond has not yet published a verified list of accepted insurance plans. Please do not assume that Beyond is in-network with your plan.

Confirmed participating plans and any relevant plan-specific details will be listed here when available. Credentialing in progress is not the same as an active participating-provider agreement.

Questions to ask your insurance plan

Is the provider in my specific network?

Ask about your exact plan and the provider, service, and location involved. A familiar insurance-company name alone does not confirm participation.

What does my plan require?

Ask whether the service requires a referral, assessment, prior authorization, or particular supporting documents.

What costs might I be responsible for?

Ask about deductibles, copayments, coinsurance, benefit limits, and any out-of-network arrangements.

Who can explain the answer in writing?

Request the relevant benefit information and keep a record of the representative’s name, the date, and the call reference number.

Coverage and payment depend on your plan and the services provided. A benefits check does not guarantee authorization or payment.

Private-pay information

Rates and payment arrangements are still being finalized. Fees and any available options will be explained before you agree to services.

Keep insurance details private

Please wait for secure intake instructions before sharing insurance cards, member numbers, or clinical records.
